# Origem Café

Identidade em espresso, papel creme e caramelo. Campanha dividida com foto de café, quadro lateral de categorias, produtos em formato de cardápio e cesta de pedidos. Fotografia de ambiente antes do rodapé em todas as páginas.

Assets: `public/store-assets/coffee-editorial-hero-v1.jpg` e `public/store-assets/coffee-house-footer-v1.jpg`. Criados pela ferramenta ImageGen integrada, convertidos de PNG para JPEG sem edição visual. Banners e slides cadastrados têm prioridade na campanha inicial.

Prompt do hero:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: editorial hero photograph for an independent Brazilian specialty coffee shop website. Primary request: a beautiful intimate coffee ritual on a dark walnut cafe counter. A warm ivory ceramic cup of espresso with rich crema on a matching saucer, a glass pour-over dripper and small matte brown coffee bag without branding in the soft background, a few roasted beans nearby. Photorealistic premium food and interior photography, natural tactile detail, inviting late-morning window light, warm espresso brown, caramel, cream and walnut palette. Composition: landscape 3:2, close medium view with cup as focal point slightly right of center, layered cozy cafe interior softly out of focus, no people, no labels. This will fill the photographic right side of a website hero; text will be rendered separately in HTML. Avoid any text, letters, logo, watermark, collage, illustrated icons, flashy colors or webpage interface.

Prompt da foto antes do rodapé:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph placed before the footer of an independent Brazilian coffee shop website. Primary request: an inviting quiet cafe corner with two warm walnut chairs and a small round wooden table beside a large softly lit window, two simple cream ceramic cups of coffee on the table, warm brown plaster wall, tasteful coffee counter softly blurred farther behind. Premium architectural lifestyle photography, authentic natural textures, soft warm late afternoon light, espresso brown, walnut, caramel and cream, grounded sophisticated atmosphere, no people. Composition wide landscape 3:2, chairs and table visible in center-right and warm dark wall on left with clean space for HTML text, realistic perspective and soft depth. No text, signs, logos, watermark, illustrated decorations, webpage interface, over-saturation.

Verificação: `scripts/verify-coffee-theme.cjs`. APIs simuladas; nenhum pedido real é enviado.
