## Prompts finais

### pet

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: pet shop website hero photograph, landscape 3:2. A joyful golden retriever beside a relaxed grey tabby cat, both healthy, natural fur detail, sitting close together in a bright warm studio. Soft cream background, small pastel aqua and peach shapes at the edges as set dressing, soft sunny lighting. Animals occupy the middle and right, enough breathing room around ears and paws. Friendly premium pet-store campaign photography. No text, no logo, no website mockup, no devices, no watermark.

### baby

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: baby boutique website hero photograph, landscape 3:2. A smiling seated baby wearing a fully covering cream knitted outfit and small knitted hat, on a soft oatmeal blanket beside a plush teddy bear. Warm caramel studio backdrop, natural soft skin texture and detailed knit fabric, gentle daylight. Subject on the right two thirds, generous calm left area for separately coded headline. Understated premium infant clothing editorial. No text, no logo, no device or website mockup, no watermark.

### fragrance

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: perfumery website hero image, landscape 3:2. Original unbranded amber glass perfume bottle with a refined bronze cap, on a textured travertine plinth, a delicate cream flower and warm brown bark in the background. Sophisticated warm sunlight, natural glass refraction, chocolate and champagne palette. Bottle on the right two thirds, calm pale warm stone negative space on the left for separately coded text. Luxury editorial still life, photorealistic. Absolutely no lettering, no brands, no logos, no website mockup, no devices, no watermark.
